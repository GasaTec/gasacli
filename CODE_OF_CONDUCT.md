# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We aim to make participation in GasaCLI welcoming and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

## Expected behavior
Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith; accept feedback; keep technical disagreements focused on evidence; respect privacy and consent.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized harassment, deliberate disruption, publishing others' private information or repeated personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban accounts, depending on severity and context. Reports will be reviewed confidentially to the extent reasonably possible.

## Reporting
Use GitHub's private security reporting for vulnerabilities. For conduct concerns, contact a repository maintainer privately through an available GitHub contact channel; a dedicated conduct reporting address must be configured before public launch. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

## Scope
This policy applies to project spaces and to public conduct representing the project.

This policy is maintained by the GasaCLI maintainers.
